[Download](https://github.com/VariantCreator/Dova-Locks/releases/latest) | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/325) | [Changelog](https://github.com/VariantCreator/Dova-Locks/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## Install

Close ICARUS and stop the server. Extract the ZIP and put `Dova-Locks_P.pak` in `Icarus/Content/Paks/mods`, replacing the old copy.

Use **1.9 on the server and every player**. Back up your prospect and A/B lock saves first, and keep those saves. Keep one active Dova copy and rebuild any merge containing it. For Jimk72's IMM, extract the PAK as a mod and rebuild your list.

## Use it

Hold **Shift + Interact** on a supported object for lock controls. Craft **Dova's Ward** where you craft the Flow Meter, then tap **Interact** on it.

Owners can use **Players** to add someone by Steam ID, even while they are offline. Use **This object** to share a chest or bench whose lock you own with selected players or everyone. Sharing allows use and taking items; pickup and lock controls keep their normal permissions.

Link your base, join up to three Wards, and choose your own boundary color and brightness. Anyone can repair; renovations need permission.
